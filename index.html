About ClassConnect

ClassConnect

Connecting students, one class at a time.

ClassConnect is a comprehensive student management platform designed to help college students stay organized, collaborate with classmates, and track their academic progress. From subject management and assignment tracking to grade calculation and classmate profiles, ClassConnect brings everything you need into one seamless app.
