Frequently Asked Questions

What is ClassConnect?
ClassConnect is a platform designed to help students connect with classmates, manage subjects, track assignments, and stay organized throughout their academic journey.
How do I create an account?
Simply click on "Sign Up" on the login page, fill in your full name, email address, and a password (at least 6 characters), then confirm your password and submit.
Can I access ClassConnect on multiple devices?
Yes! ClassConnect is a Progressive Web App (PWA) that works on both mobile phones and desktop computers. You can install it as an app for the best experience.
How do I add a subject?
Go to the Subjects page from the menu, click the "Add Subject" button, fill in the subject name, professor, and schedule, then click save.
How do I track my assignments?
Navigate to the Assignments page, click "Add Task" to create new tasks, and check them off as you complete them using the checkbox.
How does the Grades page work?
Enter your grade for each subject, select your year level and semester, and the app will automatically calculate your general average. You can also exclude subjects from the calculation.
Is my data safe?
Your data is stored locally in your browser and is not shared with anyone. We prioritize your privacy and security. For more details, please read our Privacy Policy.
Can I edit my profile information?
Yes! Go to the Profile page from the menu, update any of your personal information, and click "Save Profile" to save your changes.

About ClassConnect

ClassConnect

Connecting students, one class at a time.

ClassConnect is a comprehensive student management platform designed to help college students stay organized, collaborate with classmates, and track their academic progress. From subject management and assignment tracking to grade calculation and classmate profiles, ClassConnect brings everything you need into one seamless app.
