About ClassConnect

ClassConnect

Connecting college students, one class at a time.

ClassConnect was built by Hanz Dee Dalmino, a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology student in Cebu Technological University - Main Campus. It is a comprehensive student management platform designed to help college students stay organized, collaborate with classmates, track General Weighted Average (GWA), and store official curriculum PDF syllabi all in one place.
